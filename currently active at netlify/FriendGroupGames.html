Two games, one screen, one phone

Pass one phone around. Heads Up is the forehead-guessing game with Tamil movies (2015–now). 20 Questions hands a secret word to a "keeper" who answers yes/no while everyone else grills them.

Forehead Edition

One person holds the phone facing outward on their forehead. Everyone else describes the word (without saying it) until they guess it. Tilt the phone down for correct, up to pass — or use the on-screen buttons.

20 Questions

Pick a category, hand the phone to the "keeper." They read the word secretly, then everyone else asks yes/no questions to figure it out.

Wavelength

One team sees a hidden target on a slider between two opposites ("cold ↔ hot"); they give a one-word clue and the rest guess where the dial lands.

Taboo

Guess the word from a teammate's clues — without them using five forbidden related words. Great with the same phone-timer format as Heads Up.

Fishbowl

Same word set, three rounds: describe it, act it out (charades), then say one word only — memory carries it home.

Most Likely To

Read a prompt ("most likely to become famous"), everyone points at once — funniest for a full 6-person circle, no setup needed.

Category Blurt

Shout out items in a category ("Rajinikanth movies") in 30 seconds without repeating — last one standing wins the round.

How to Play

Classic Mode - 3×3 Grid: Get 3 of your marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to win.

Classic Mode - 5×5 & 7×7 Grids: Get 4 of your marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) to win.

♾️ Never Ends Mode: No draws, ever. Each player only gets a limited number of pieces to place - exactly as many as needed to form a winning line (3 pieces each on 3×3, 4 pieces each on 5×5 and 7×7). Since your pieces are always fewer than the empty cells, the board can never fill up completely.

Place your pieces one at a time, taking turns. Once both players have placed all their pieces, the Movement Phase begins: instead of placing new marks, click one of your own pieces to select it (it glows gold), then click any empty cell to slide it there. Keep sliding pieces on your turn until someone lines up 3 (or 4) in a row - the game literally cannot end in a draw.

Player X always starts first. Click on any empty cell to place your mark. Use Undo to take back the last move in either mode.
